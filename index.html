the world is made of chunks

Minecraft loads its world in chunks: 16x16 block columns reaching from a height of Y −64 up to Y 319. This one holds 32,383 blocks, not counting air. What lies beneath the surface? Let’s take it apart, layer by layer.

LAYER 1

Dirt

Beneath the grass lies a layer of soil only a few blocks thick. More dirt hides as blobs deep inside the stone.

LAYER 2

Stone

The base rock of the upper world, laced with blobs of granite, diorite, andesite and gravel, and with the common ores: coal, copper and iron.

LAYER 3

Deepslate

From Y 8 downward, stone gradually gives way to deepslate; below Y 0 there is nothing else. This is where tuff and the deep ores sit: gold, redstone, lapis and diamond.

LAYER 4

Bedrock

The floor of the world. Unbreakable, with a ragged pattern that thins out toward Y −60.
